# Code of Conduct

JST Stack follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not accepted in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to [@antonbelous0v](https://github.com/antonbelous0v). Reports are reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently exclude participants when needed to protect the community.

## Scope

This policy applies to JST Stack repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related public interactions.
